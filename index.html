LEGAL SUPPORT

Free legal aid may be available to you.

India's legal services authorities provide free legal services to people who meet the eligibility criteria under the Legal Services Authorities Act. Eligibility can depend on factors such as category, income and the nature of the case.

Visit NALSA official website ↗

How to get started

  1. Check eligibilityRead the current criteria on the official legal services website.
  2. Contact the authorityApproach your District Legal Services Authority (DLSA), Taluk Legal Services Committee or State Legal Services Authority.
  3. Explain your issueShare relevant facts and documents with the legal services authority.
  4. Follow official guidanceAsk about the application process and next steps for your matter.

ONLINE SAFETY

Cybercrime or online financial fraud

  • For financial cyber fraud, contact your bank/payment provider immediately.
  • Keep transaction IDs, screenshots, phone numbers and messages.
  • Report through the official National Cyber Crime Reporting Portal.
Open cybercrime portal ↗
CONSUMER SUPPORT

Product or service complaint

  • Keep bills, order details, warranty and communication records.
  • First contact the seller or service provider and keep the response.
  • Use the National Consumer Helpline for grievance guidance.
Open consumer helpline ↗
SAFETY & PROTECTION

Immediate safety concern

  • If someone is in immediate danger, call India's emergency number 112.
  • Move to a safer place if possible and contact a trusted person.
  • For legal support, contact the relevant legal services authority.

QUICK ANSWERS

Frequently asked questions

Is legal aid really free?

Free legal services are available to people who qualify under the law. Contact an official legal services authority to confirm eligibility and the process for your situation.

Can this website provide legal advice?

No. NyayaSetu is an educational student project. It provides general awareness and links to official services, not individual legal advice or representation.

What should I do if I do not know which authority to contact?

You can start with NALSA or your State/District Legal Services Authority and explain the issue. They can guide you to the appropriate service.

Do I need to upload personal documents here?

No. This website does not collect legal case documents or sensitive personal information. Share documents only through verified official channels when required.

Important notice
